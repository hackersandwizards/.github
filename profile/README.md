### Your whole company unstoppable, in flow with agentic engineering.

hackers&wizards trains and mentors engineering and product teams, their leaders, and everyone who works around them. Most trainings are hands-on days on the client's own codebase, remote or on site across Germany, Austria and Switzerland, in German or English.

The repositories here hold the training applications participants work in, and tools we build for our own agentic engineering, such as MCP servers and a terminal setup.

Stefan Munz and Benedikt Stemmildt lead every engagement from Hamburg, and trainers from a network of more than 70 practitioners deliver it.

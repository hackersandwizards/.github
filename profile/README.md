### Agentic engineering training and mentoring for engineering and product teams

hackers&wizards from Hamburg trains and mentors engineering and product teams and their leaders in agentic engineering, for companies whose developers have coding agents but whose releases have not sped up. The trainings are hands-on days on your own codebase, in the coding agent your team already uses, remote or on site across Germany, Austria and Switzerland, in German or English. Every two-day training comes with weeks of mentoring while the team applies it to its own work.

Stefan Munz and Benedikt Stemmildt lead every engagement, with trainers from a network of 70+ practitioners. We have built large software systems for more than 20 years and have worked with coding agents since the end of 2022.

Teams we have trained include dm, IONOS, Krombacher and LichtBlick, and by September 2026, 644 developers held a training certificate from us. Benedikt chairs the program of [code.talks](https://codetalks.com/) and co-wrote _Building the Agentic Software Factory_ (Packt).

#### What you find here

- [artificer](https://github.com/hackersandwizards/artificer): a kit for building small software factories on Claude Code from gated agent steps you can read end to end.
- [hycle](https://github.com/hackersandwizards/hycle): our hypothesis on how work shifts when agents make building cheap (German).
- [bench](https://github.com/hackersandwizards/bench): the terminal setup we work in, for macOS on Apple Silicon.
- [agentic-engineering-training-nextjs](https://github.com/hackersandwizards/agentic-engineering-training-nextjs): a small CRM that training participants practise on.
